A few quick answers about my work, process, pricing and what it’s like to work with me.

A few quick answers about my work, process, pricing and what it’s like to work with me.

What do you do?

I design and build modern digital experiences, combining strategy, psychology, design and technology to create websites and digital products that are both useful and visually considered.

What kind of projects do you work on?

I primarily work on websites, landing pages, UI/UX, branding, digital experiences and custom development for individuals, startups and businesses.

How much does a website cost?

My website projects currently start at ₹10,000, with more comprehensive projects starting around ₹20,000. The final price depends on the scope, pages, functionality and level of customization.

Do you offer ongoing website maintenance?

Yes. I offer monthly website maintenance for updates, content changes, bug fixes, design adjustments, performance checks and general technical support.

Do I work directly with you?

Yes. I handle the strategy, design and development directly, so you have a single point of contact throughout the project.

How long does a typical project take?

Most smaller projects take around 1–2 weeks, while larger or more involved projects can take 2–4 weeks, depending on the scope and feedback cycle.
